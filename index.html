PUBLICATION: OPTIMIZER ENERGY EFFICIENCY STUDY

SEPT 2025

An Analysis of Optimizer Choice on Energy Efficiency and Performance in Neural Network Training

Comprehensive empirical study investigating the relationship between optimizer choice and energy efficiency in neural network training. Conducted 360 controlled experiments across three benchmark datasets (MNIST, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100) using eight popular optimizers. Using CodeCarbon for precise energy tracking on Apple M1 Pro hardware, measured training duration, peak memory usage, CO2 emissions, and final model performance. Identified AdamW and NAdam as consistently efficient choices, while SGD demonstrates superior performance on complex datasets despite higher emissions.

Status: Submitted to Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems (Elsevier), Manuscript ID: SUSCOM-D-25-02415, under review.
